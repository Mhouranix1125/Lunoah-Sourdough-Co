# Your Lunoah Website: How To

Everything happens on one page: **your website address + `/admin`**
(for example `lunoahsourdough.com/admin`). It works on your phone or computer.

Log in with your email and password. You'll stay logged in for 30 days.

---

## Add a new bread
1. Tap **＋ Add a new bread**
2. Tap the photo box to take a picture or pick one from your photos
3. Type the name, price and a short description
4. Tap **Save**

It shows up on the website right away.

## Change a price, name, description or photo
Tap **Edit** next to the bread, make your change, tap **Save**.

## Sold out for the day
Flip the **Sold out** switch. The bread stays on the website with a "Sold out" label,
and customers can't add it to an order. Flip it back when you're baking it again.

## Take a bread off the website (without deleting it)
**Edit** → check **Hide from website** → **Save**. Uncheck it anytime to bring it back.

## Delete a bread forever
**Edit** → **Delete this bread forever** at the bottom.

## Change the order breads appear in
Use the **▲ ▼** arrows.

## Change your phone number, pickup times, or the words on the front page
Tap **Shop Info**, make your changes, tap **Save shop info**.
You can also add a big photo for the top of the website there.

## Payment apps (Venmo, Zelle, Cash App…)
Tap **Payments**. Your website shows each app's logo under **How to pay**. Customers tap a
logo to see your QR code, username or number.
- **Change a username, number or QR code:** tap **Edit** next to the app, change it, tap **Save**.
  For the QR code, open the app (e.g. Venmo → your QR code), take a screenshot, and choose that screenshot.
- **Add another app:** tap **＋ Add a payment app** and pick it (Cash App, PayPal, Apple Cash, or Other,
  e.g. "Cash at pickup").
- **Hide one for now:** flip its **Show** switch off.
- **Remove one:** **Edit** → **Remove this payment method**.

## How orders reach you
Customers tap **+** on the breads they want, then **Checkout**:
1. **Your order:** their name and pickup day
2. **Pay:** they pick Venmo, Zelle, etc., pay the total, and tick "I've sent $__".
   Venmo opens with the amount and the order code already filled in.
3. **Send order:** their phone opens a text to your order number with everything filled in.

The text you get looks like:
```
Hi Lunoah! Order LUN-4821
• 2 × Seeded Whole Wheat ($24)
Total: $24
Paid: $24 via Venmo (from Sam R)
Name: Sam Rivera
Pickup: Saturday
```
**Before baking, check the money arrived:** open Venmo or your bank's Zelle and look for a payment
of that amount from that name, with the order code in the note. The website can't check this for you.
Then reply to the text to confirm.

**Want to take payment at pickup instead?** **Shop Info** → turn off
"Customers must pay before sending their order" → **Save**.

## Forgot your password?
Ask your website helper to reset it. It takes them a minute.

**Tip:** Photos look best taken in daylight, from slightly above, on a plain background.
